NEON PROMOTER

NEON PROMOTER is a private desktop software project for creating and managing organic Pinterest Pins promoting original print-on-demand designs sold on TeePublic.

The planned application will help the account owner prepare Pin images, titles, descriptions, and direct product links, publish Pins to the owner's Pinterest boards upon authorization, and review relevant Pinterest performance metrics.

The application is currently under development. It is designed for use with the owner's own Pinterest Business account, not for managing other users' accounts or paid advertising.

Privacy Policy
